# clinical-guidelines-reference-v3.md
## Core Clinical Guidelines (1-43) vs. Adapted African Practice

### 🏷️ Guideline 1: World Health Organization (WHO) — Emergency Care Toolkit, Basic Emergency Care, trauma, infectious disease, mass-casualty and resource-limited emergency care guidance.
*   **Global Standard**: Aggressive, resource-intensive time-to-treatment targets, routine lab screening, and advanced diagnostics.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Severe shortages of human resources, lack of blood culture bottles, lactate assays, or ICU ventilators [24, 71].
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Implementation of the qSOFA and SATS (South African Triage Scale) for clinical risk stratification. Standardizing 'Assess, Stabilise, Administer, Prioritise' (ASAP) protocols to prioritize high-risk syndromic cohorts.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: FEAST Trial (2011) demonstrating 45% increased mortality with aggressive rapid fluid boluses in resource-limited pediatric sepsis.
*   **Active Bibliography**: World Health Organization. Pocket book of hospital care for children: Guidelines for the management of common childhood illnesses. 2nd edition, Geneva: World Health Organization; 2013.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://tbksp.who.int/en/node/2141](https://tbksp.who.int/en/node/2141)

---
### 🏷️ Guideline 2: International Federation for Emergency Medicine (IFEM) — international ED standards, quality and safety frameworks, paediatric ED standards and disaster guidance.
*   **Global Standard**: Rigid facility-level structural requirements, specific ratios of emergency specialists, and specialized pediatric resuscitation zones.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Widespread rural primary care settings where emergency units are run by general medical officers without emergency specialty training, facing unstable electrical grids and variable oxygen supply.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Focus on standardized basic clinical pathways, task-sharing, and regional simulation-based training (e.g., ESMOE/EOST) rather than fixed physical infrastructure.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: Baelani I, et al. Availability of critical care resources survey.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Baelani I, Jochberger S, Laimer T, et al. Availability of critical care resources to treat patients with severe sepsis or septic shock in Africa: a self-reported survey of anaesthesia providers. Critical Care. 2011;15(1):R10.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://anaesthetics.ukzn.ac.za/Libraries/Documents2011/Availability_of_Critical_care_Resources.pdf](https://anaesthetics.ukzn.ac.za/Libraries/Documents2011/Availability_of_Critical_care_Resources.pdf)

### 🏷️ Guideline 3: American College of Emergency Physicians (ACEP) — clinical policies specifically addressing ED patients.
*   **Global Standard**: Rapid diagnostic workups with high-sensitivity serum biomarkers (such as highly sensitive troponins) and immediate CT scans.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Extremely slow lab turnaround times and lack of CT imaging after hours in district and regional clinics.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Heuristics and diagnostic algorithms based on clinical scores (e.g., Wells score for PE, qSOFA for infection, and ABC score for massive transfusion).
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: Andrews B, et al. Early resuscitation protocol randomized trial.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Andrews B, Semler MW, Muchemwa L, et al. Effect of an early resuscitation protocol on in-hospital mortality among adults with sepsis and hypotension: a randomized clinical trial. JAMA. 2017;318(13):1233-1240.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3515744/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3515744/)

### 🏷️ Guideline 4: Royal College of Emergency Medicine (RCEM) — clinical standards covering resuscitation, sedation, trauma, paediatrics, infectious disease and general ED care.
*   **Global Standard**: Strict quality targets including rapid time-to-treatment windows and specific mechanical ventilation standards.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Severe shortage of critical care infrastructure, where mechanical ventilators are highly scarce and ICU beds are severely limited.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Prioritization of non-invasive ventilatory support (like nasal prongs or face masks) and careful volume-targeted fluid resuscitations.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: Mthatha Regional Hospital prospective observational pragmatic cohort study.
*   **Active Bibliography**: One in four die from acute infectious illness in an emergency department in Eastern Cape Province, South Africa. S Afr Med J. 2020;111(2):e12.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://scielo.org.za/pdf/samj/v111n2/12.pdf](https://scielo.org.za/pdf/samj/v111n2/12.pdf)

### 🏷️ Guideline 5: National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE) — condition-specific and service-delivery guidance covering most major ED presentations.
*   **Global Standard**: Comprehensive service-delivery models with specialized rapid referral networks and direct-to-specialist pathways.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Long travel distances, prolonged transfer delays, and lack of specialized regional centers.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Strengthening primary stabilization and 'holding phase' protocols within district hospitals to support patients safely for 24-72 hours before transfer.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: NICE Bacterial Meningitis Guideline South African adaptation.
*   **Active Bibliography**: FINAL DRAFT Paediatric Clinical Protocol - Western Cape Government. Paediatric Meningitis Protocol. 2017.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://www.westerncape.gov.za/health-wellness/files/wcg-blob-files?file=2025-08/meningitis_manag.pdf&type=file](https://www.westerncape.gov.za/health-wellness/files/wcg-blob-files?file=2025-08/meningitis_manag.pdf&type=file)

### 🏷️ Guideline 6: American College of Radiology Appropriateness Criteria (ACR-AC) — evidence-based selection of imaging for specific clinical presentations.
*   **Global Standard**: Routine high-resolution Computed Tomography (CT) or Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) as first-line modalities for acute abdominal or chest pain.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Extremely limited access to CT and zero routine emergency MRI. Imaging is frequently restricted to plain film radiography and basic bedside ultrasound.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Meticulous training in Point-of-Care Ultrasound (POCUS) and Focused Assessment with Sonography for Trauma (FAST) to guide surgical or resuscitation decisions.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: Wood D, et al. Ultrasound findings in cytotoxic snakebites.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Wood D, Sartorius B, Hift R. Ultrasound findings in 42 patients with cytotoxic tissue damage following bites by South African snakes. Emerg Med J. 2016;33(7):477-481.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://journals.co.za/doi/10.7196/SAMJ.2023.v113i6.1037](https://journals.co.za/doi/10.7196/SAMJ.2023.v113i6.1037)

### 🏷️ Guideline 7: International Liaison Committee on Resuscitation (ILCOR) — international evidence reviews and Consensus on Science with Treatment Recommendations.
*   **Global Standard**: Highly standardized, advanced cardiac life support protocols with post-cardiac arrest targeted temperature management (TTM).
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Inability to perform advanced thermoregulation or targeted therapeutic cooling due to lack of cooling blankets and ICU monitoring equipment.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Focus on high-quality basic CPR, rapid defibrillation, and active avoidance of hyperthermia using basic physical methods (such as tepid sponging).
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: WSES guidelines for management of surgical sepsis.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Sartelli M, Kluger Y, Anseloni L, et al. Raising concerns about the Sepsis-3 definitions. World J Emerg Surg. 2018;13:6.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://scielo.org.za/scielo.php?script=sci_arttext&pid=S0038-23612019000400003](https://scielo.org.za/scielo.php?script=sci_arttext&pid=S0038-23612019000400003)

### 🏷️ Guideline 8: European Resuscitation Council (ERC) Guidelines 2025 — adult, paediatric and neonatal resuscitation, post-resuscitation care and special circumstances.
*   **Global Standard**: Advanced algorithm-driven resuscitations including real-time end-tidal CO2 (EtCO2) feedback and immediate post-resuscitation coronary angiography.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Lack of quantitative EtCO2 waveform monitoring and absence of after-hours catheterization laboratories.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Clinical indicators of tube placement (such as bilateral chest expansion, misting of the tube, and clinical surrogates) and immediate medical stabilization.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: SASS Consensus Guidelines.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Approach to the diagnosis and management of snakebite envenomation in South Africa in humans. S Afr Med J. 2023;113(6):e1037.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://knowledgehub.health.gov.za/system/files/2024-10/Hardcastle%20and%20SASS_Snakebite%20guidelines%20in%20hospital_SAMJ%20CME%202023.pdf](https://knowledgehub.health.gov.za/system/files/2024-10/Hardcastle%20and%20SASS_Snakebite%20guidelines%20in%20hospital_SAMJ%20CME%202023.pdf)

### 🏷️ Guideline 9: American Heart Association CPR and Emergency Cardiovascular Care Guidelines 2025 — BLS, ACLS, PALS, neonatal resuscitation and systems of care.
*   **Global Standard**: Mandates real-time CPR quality feedback devices and advanced pediatric post-resuscitation algorithms.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: No access to CPR feedback metrics and severe limitations in pediatric intensive care capacity.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Structured, team-based simulation training and task-sharing to maximize CPR quality through manual observation and strict rotation schedules.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: ESMOE Emergency Obstetric Simulation Training (EOST).
*   **Active Bibliography**: Maternal Collapse: Training in resuscitation. Essential Steps in Managing Obstetric Emergencies (ESMOE). ResearchGate. 2015.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://www.researchgate.net/publication/281054365_Maternal_Collapse_Training_in_resuscitation](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/281054365_Maternal_Collapse_Training_in_resuscitation)

### 🏷️ Guideline 10: Surviving Sepsis Campaign — adult and paediatric sepsis and septic shock.
*   **Global Standard**: Rigid 1-hour resuscitation bundle prescribing a minimum of 30 mL/kg of crystalloid fluid within the first 3 hours [153].
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: The FEAST trial showed that rapid fluid boluses increase relative 48-hour pediatric mortality by 45% in low-resource settings lacking mechanical ventilation [154].
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Replacing the 1-hour bundle with the ASAP (Assess, Stabilise, Administer, Prioritise) protocol. Initial fluid volumes are restricted to small, targeted boluses (4 mL/kg or 250-500 mL) [156].
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: FEAST Trial (2011) demonstrating 45% increased mortality with rapid fluid boluses.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Maitland K, Kiguli S, Opoka RO, et al. Mortality after fluid bolus in African children with severe infection (FEAST). N Engl J Med. 2011;364(26):2483-2495.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202601.0435](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202601.0435)

---
### 🏷️ Guideline 11: Society of Critical Care Medicine (SCCM) — airway, sedation, shock, sepsis, analgesia and other critical-care guidance applicable during ED resuscitation.
*   **Global Standard**: Routine utilization of continuous intravenous anesthetic infusions and objective sedation scales in ventilated patients.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Extremely limited supply of continuous infusion pumps and lack of dedicated critical care nursing staff.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Intermittent bolus dosing of sedation (e.g., titrated ketamine 1-2 mg/kg/hour or diazepam) and robust visual clinical monitoring [54].
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: SASS Consensus Guidelines.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Approach to the diagnosis and management of snakebite envenomation in South Africa in humans. S Afr Med J. 2023;113(6):e1037.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://knowledgehub.health.gov.za/system/files/2024-10/Hardcastle%20and%20SASS_Snakebite%20guidelines%20in%20hospital_SAMJ%20CME%202023.pdf](https://knowledgehub.health.gov.za/system/files/2024-10/Hardcastle%20and%20SASS_Snakebite%20guidelines%20in%20hospital_SAMJ%20CME%202023.pdf)

### 🏷️ Guideline 12: European Society of Intensive Care Medicine (ESICM) — critical-care guidelines and international consensus statements.
*   **Global Standard**: Mandatory continuous central venous pressure (CVP) and arterial line monitoring to guide shock therapy.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Lack of invasive pressure monitoring transducers and arterial cannulas in rural or secondary emergency settings.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Using dynamic bedside clinical assessments, such as the Passive Leg Raise (PLR) test (measuring change in pulse pressure) and capillary refill time [155].
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: ANDROMEDA-SHOCK Randomized Clinical Trial.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Evans L, Rhodes A, Alhazzani W, et al. Surviving Sepsis Campaign: International Guidelines for Management of Sepsis and Septic Shock 2021. Crit Care Med. 2021;49(11):e55.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8486643/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8486643/)

### 🏷️ Guideline 13: European Society of Cardiology (ESC) — acute coronary syndromes, pulmonary embolism, aortic disease, arrhythmias, heart failure and hypertensive emergencies.
*   **Global Standard**: Immediate primary percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI) within 90-120 minutes of STEMI diagnosis.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Absence of coronary catheterization facilities in the public sector outside major metropolitan tertiary centers.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Rapid administration of pharmacological fibrinolysis (such as Tenecteplase or Streptokinase) in the ED within 30 minutes, followed by planned transfer.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: Southern African Hypertension Society 2014 Guidelines.
*   **Active Bibliography**: South African hypertension practice guideline 2014. Cardiovasc J Afr. 2014;25(6):288-294.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4327181/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4327181/)

### 🏷️ Guideline 14: American Heart Association/American College of Cardiology (AHA/ACC) — acute cardiovascular disease, arrhythmias, chest pain, pulmonary embolism and heart failure.
*   **Global Standard**: Mandates high-sensitivity troponin assays and rapid-rule-out chest pain pathways (0-hour and 1-hour protocols).
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Turnaround times for serum cardiac markers are frequently 4-12 hours, rendering rapid-rule-out algorithms clinically dangerous.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Adopting the HEART score with standard point-of-care troponin checks and serial ECG monitoring to safely risk-stratify chest pain.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: SA Hypertension Practice Guideline 2014.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Seedat YK, Rayner BL. South African hypertension practice guideline 2014. Cardiovasc J Afr. 2014;25(6):288-294.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4327181/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4327181/)

### 🏷️ Guideline 15: American Heart Association/American Stroke Association (AHA/ASA) — acute ischaemic stroke, intracerebral haemorrhage and subarachnoid haemorrhage; the acute ischaemic stroke guideline was updated in 2026.
*   **Global Standard**: Thrombolytic therapy (Alteplase) within 4.5 hours of ischemic stroke onset, and immediate endovascular thrombectomy (EVT) for large vessel occlusions.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Extremely limited access to emergency head CT scans after hours and near-total absence of EVT facilities in public hospitals.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Meticulous clinical stroke screening, rapid blood glucose checks to rule out mimic hypoglycemia, and strict SBP management (<185/110 mmHg for thrombolysis candidates).
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: AHA/ASA blood pressure management guidelines.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Hypertensive Emergencies - ACCP. Clinical Guidelines. 2018.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://www.accp.com/docs/bookstore/ccsap/ccsap2018b1_sample.pdf](https://www.accp.com/docs/bookstore/ccsap/ccsap2018b1_sample.pdf)

---
### 🏷️ Guideline 16: CHEST Guidelines — venous thromboembolism, pulmonary embolism and antithrombotic treatment.
*   **Global Standard**: Routine low-molecular-weight heparin (LMWH) for VTE prophylaxis in all hospitalized medical patients.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: High risk of bleeding in patients with undetected parasitic infections, chronic tuberculosis, or advanced HIV disease.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Strict adherence to VTE risk-stratification models, starting prophylactic enoxaparin (40 mg daily) only after ruling out active hemoptysis or bleeding.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: Pediatric Diabetes guidelines.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Guidelines and Protocols of Diabetes Emergencies. Pediatric Protocol. Pediatric Diabetes. 2014.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://www.moh.gov.sa/Documents/Diabetes-Emergencies.pdf](https://www.moh.gov.sa/Documents/Diabetes-Emergencies.pdf)

---
### 🏷️ Guideline 17: American Society of Hematology (ASH) — venous thromboembolism, anticoagulation and haematological emergencies.
*   **Global Standard**: Routine use of direct oral anticoagulants (DOACs) as first-line therapy for acute deep vein thrombosis.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: DOACs are highly expensive and virtually unavailable on the public hospital essential medicines list.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Utilizing standard Warfarin therapy, bridging initially with subcutaneous Enoxaparin, and relying on clinic-based INR monitoring.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: SA National Health EML.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Standard Treatment Guidelines and Essential Medicines List for South Africa: hospital level, adults, 2024 edition. National Department of Health; 2024.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://sahivsoc.org/Files/SAHCS%20crytococcal%20meningitis%20guideline%20update%202026.pdf](https://sahivsoc.org/Files/SAHCS%20crytococcal%20meningitis%20guideline%20update%202026.pdf)

### 🏷️ Guideline 18: American College of Surgeons Trauma Quality Programs Best Practices Guidelines — trauma resuscitation, traumatic brain injury, geriatric trauma and trauma-system care.
*   **Global Standard**: Mandates immediate trauma center transfer, massive transfusion protocols guided by thromboelastography (TEG), and rapid CT scanning.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: No access to TEG/ROTEM in secondary EDs. Red blood cell and plasma products must be rationed carefully due to chronic blood shortages.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Relying on the clinical Assessment of Blood Consumption (ABC) score (scoring mechanism) to trigger MTP, and administering standard 1:1:1 ratios of blood products.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: CRASH-2 Trial and standard Damage Control Resuscitation guidelines.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Damage Control Resuscitation. PMC - NIH. Journal of Trauma. 2009;23:231-40.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7120797/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7120797/)

### 🏷️ Guideline 19: Eastern Association for the Surgery of Trauma (EAST) — evidence-based trauma practice-management guidelines.
*   **Global Standard**: Universal CT angiography or mandatory surgical exploration for any neck injury that penetrates the platysma.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Mandatory exploration of all platysma-penetrating neck wounds leads to a high rate of non-therapeutic surgeries.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Adopting Selective Non-Operative Management (SNOM). Surgical exploration is restricted to patients with clinical 'hard signs' of vascular or aerodigestive damage (e.g., active bleeding, expanding hematoma, air bubbling) [126, 158].
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: Penetrating neck trauma audit at Chris Hani Baragwanath Academic Hospital.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Surgical exploration for penetrating neck trauma - an audit of results in 145 patients. South African Journal of Surgery. 2020;57(4).
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://journals.co.za/doi/abs/10.36303/SAJS.4020](https://journals.co.za/doi/abs/10.36303/SAJS.4020)

---
### 🏷️ Guideline 20: World Society of Emergency Surgery (WSES) — emergency general surgery, intra-abdominal infection, appendicitis, trauma and source control.
*   **Global Standard**: Immediate laparoscopy and advanced intra-abdominal source control with high-generation intravenous antibiotics.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Severe theater backlogs and delayed access to emergency surgery, often with wait times exceeding 12-24 hours for acute appendicitis.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Early administration of standard broad-spectrum antibiotics (such as Ceftriaxone and Metronidazole) and aggressive physiological resuscitation while awaiting theater.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: WSES guidelines for management of intra-abdominal infections.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Compliance with the Surviving Sepsis Campaign guidelines for early resuscitation does not translate into improved outcomes in patients with surgical sepsis in South Africa. S Afr J Surg. 2019;57(4).
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://scielo.org.za/scielo.php?script=sci_arttext&pid=S0038-23612019000400003](https://scielo.org.za/scielo.php?script=sci_arttext&pid=S0038-23612019000400003)

### 🏷️ Guideline 21: American Association for the Surgery of Trauma (AAST) — often produces joint trauma guidelines with WSES.
*   **Global Standard**: Recommends angiographic embolization for hemodynamically stable high-grade splenic or hepatic lacerations.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Angiographic embolization is completely unavailable in the vast majority of African public secondary hospitals.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Rigid clinical observation in a high-care unit with serial hemoglobin monitoring and a low threshold for open emergency splenectomy if deterioration occurs.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: Damage Control Resuscitation.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Update on massive transfusion. British Journal of Anaesthesia. 2013;112(1).
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://anaesthetics.ukzn.ac.za/Libraries/TRAUMA/Massive_transfusion_Br_J_Anaesth_-2013.pdf](https://anaesthetics.ukzn.ac.za/Libraries/TRAUMA/Massive_transfusion_Br_J_Anaesth_-2013.pdf)

### 🏷️ Guideline 22: Brain Trauma Foundation (BTF) — adult and paediatric severe traumatic brain injury, penetrating TBI and prehospital neurotrauma.
*   **Global Standard**: Mandates intracranial pressure (ICP) monitor placement for all patients with severe TBI (GCS < 9) and abnormal CT scans.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: ICP monitors are completely unavailable in almost all district and regional emergency departments.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Relying on clinical neuro-checks and administering hyperosmolar rescue therapy (3% hypertonic saline 3 mL/kg or 20% mannitol) only if active brain herniation occurs.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: Meningitis and Brain Injury guidelines.
*   **Active Bibliography**: FINAL DRAFT Paediatric Clinical Protocol - Western Cape Government. Paediatric Meningitis Protocol. 2017.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://www.westerncape.gov.za/health-wellness/files/wcg-blob-files?file=2025-08/meningitis_manag.pdf&type=file](https://www.westerncape.gov.za/health-wellness/files/wcg-blob-files?file=2025-08/meningitis_manag.pdf&type=file)

### 🏷️ Guideline 23: Global Initiative for Asthma (GINA) — acute asthma assessment and management; current strategy report is GINA 2026.
*   **Global Standard**: Frequent use of high-dose inhaled corticosteroids, rapid peak flow measurements, and early non-invasive positive pressure ventilation.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Lack of peak flow meters in emergency units and high risk of gastric inflation with non-invasive ventilation.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Relying on simple clinical signs (respiratory rate, accessory muscle use, SpO2) and standard hourly beta-2 agonist nebulizations combined with oral prednisone.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: Standard Pneumothorax guidelines.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Pneumothorax - The Art of Urgent Care. Ghost. 2024.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://art-of-urgent-care.ghost.io/pneumothorax/](https://art-of-urgent-care.ghost.io/pneumothorax/)

### 🏷️ Guideline 24: Global Initiative for Chronic Obstructive Lung Disease (GOLD) — COPD diagnosis, exacerbations, ventilation and ongoing management; current report is GOLD 2026.
*   **Global Standard**: First-line non-invasive positive pressure ventilation (BiPAP) for acute hypercapnic respiratory failure.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Severe shortage of BiPAP machines in district hospitals, where hypercapnic patients must often be managed with titrated oxygen.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Extremely cautious oxygen titration via nasal prongs to a strict target of 88-92% to avoid suppressing the hypoxic respiratory drive.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: Pneumothorax and COPD guidelines.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Conservative management of spontaneous pneumothorax: A review of evidence and guidelines. PMC. 2025.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12208270/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12208270/)

### 🏷️ Guideline 25: European Respiratory Society/American Thoracic Society (ERS/ATS) — acute respiratory failure, non-invasive ventilation, severe asthma and respiratory infections.
*   **Global Standard**: Mandatory high-flow nasal cannula (HFNC) therapy and continuous arterial blood gas (ABG) monitoring.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: HFNC systems are unavailable in most district hospitals, and ABG syringes or machines are scarce.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Relying on standard low-flow oxygen devices (nasal prongs, non-rebreather face masks) and monitoring gas exchange via pulse oximetry.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: Sepsis bundle adaptations.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Adapting the Sepsis 1-Hour Bundle for Resource-Limited Settings: Lessons from Turkana, Kenya. Preprints.org. 2026.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202601.0435](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202601.0435)

### 🏷️ Guideline 26: British Thoracic Society/Scottish Intercollegiate Guidelines Network (BTS/SIGN) — asthma, pleural disease, oxygen therapy and pneumothorax guidance.
*   **Global Standard**: The BTS 2023 update de-emphasizes radiographic size thresholds, recommending conservative management for stable Primary Spontaneous Pneumothorax (PSP) [179].
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: In rural district hospitals, commercial thoracostomy sets are often out of stock, making invasive chest drain insertions difficult.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Stable PSP is observed conservatively with simple analgesia [179]. For symptomatic PSP, manual needle aspiration via a three-way tap is the first-line rescue.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: BTS 2023 Guidelines.
*   **Active Bibliography**: Conservative management of spontaneous pneumothorax: A review of evidence and guidelines. Bioinformation. 2025;21(3):471-484.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12208270/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12208270/)

### 🏷️ Guideline 27: Infectious Diseases Society of America (IDSA) — meningitis, pneumonia, urinary infection, skin infection, antimicrobial resistance and infection-specific guidance.
*   **Global Standard**: Immediate lumbar puncture followed by empiric administration of third-generation cephalosporins, vancomycin, and ampicillin for suspected bacterial meningitis.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: High baseline rates of HIV and cryptococcal disease. Empiric antibacterial therapy fails to cover cryptococcal meningitis, which is highly prevalent [46].
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Performing rapid Cryptococcal Antigen (CrAg) lateral flow assays on all CSF samples (or serum) and deferring broad-spectrum antibacterial steroids.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: AMBITION-cm trial and SAHCS Guidelines.
*   **Active Bibliography**: SAHCS Guideline for the prevention, diagnosis and management of cryptococcal disease among persons living with HIV: Update to in. Southern African HIV Clinicians Society. 2026.
*   **Direct Source URL**: [https://sahivsoc.org/Files/SAHCS%20crytococcal%20meningitis%20guideline%20update%202026.pdf](https://sahivsoc.org/Files/SAHCS%20crytococcal%20meningitis%20guideline%20update%202026.pdf)

### 🏷️ Guideline 28: European Society of Clinical Microbiology and Infectious Diseases (ESCMID) — severe infection, antimicrobial stewardship and resistant organisms, including ED-specific stewardship guidance.
*   **Global Standard**: Mandates patient-specific microbiological cultures and restricted, narrow-spectrum step-down antibiotic therapy.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: Microbiological culture results are severely delayed (often taking 5-7 days), making rapid clinical step-down impossible.
*   **The Local Adaptation ("What to say on rounds")**: Strict adherence to regional empiric syndromic antibiotic guidelines (e.g., Ceftriaxone for severe community-acquired infections) to cover highly resistant pathogens.
*   **Landmark Supporting Evidence**: Sepsis mortality audits.
*   **Active Bibliography**: One in four die from acute infectious illness in an emergency department in Eastern Cape Province, South Africa. S Afr Med J. 2020;111(2):e12.

### 🏷️ Guideline 41: American Academy of Clinical Toxicology (AACT) — toxicological position statements and decontamination guidance.
*   **Global Standard**: Universal administration of activated charcoal (1 g/kg) within 1 hour of any potentially toxic ingestion.
*   **The African Reality / Physiological Conflict**: In South African primary care, oral N-acetylcysteine (NAC) is used for paracetamol poisonings when referrals are delayed [3]. Activated charcoal binds oral NAC, negating its therapeutic effect [4].
